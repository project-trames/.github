# Code of Conduct

Trames is dedicated to providing a respectful, productive, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

We believe that open systems software thrives on rigorous, evidence-based engineering. Participants must be able to explore ideas, report flaws, critique code, and respectfully disagree without fear of harassment.

---

## Expected Standards

* **Focus on Code and Evidence**: Discuss architecture, technical trade-offs, and empirical results. Critique ideas and implementations, not the people behind them.
* **Welcome Technical Debate**: Rigorous technical disagreement and good-faith critique are encouraged. Concurring with maintainers is never a prerequisite for participating.
* **Respect Boundaries and Time**: Everyone participates on a finite schedule. Avoid badgering, unsolicited repetitive pings, or demanding immediate reviews or releases.
* **Be Constructive**: When reporting issues or reviewing PRs, provide actionable context, verify what you observed versus what you inferred, and accept corrections gracefully.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any project space:

* Harassment, hate speech, derogatory language, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained derailment of technical discussions.
* Doxxing or publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
* Abusive use of automated bots, scripts, or AI coding agents to flood issue queues, evade moderation, or spam community channels.
* Retaliation against anyone who reports an issue or assists in moderation.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Trames project, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and community discussions. It also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public forums.

### Enforcement

Maintainers reserve the right to take proportionate action to preserve community health, including:

1. **Correction**: Private guidance or content moderation for minor infractions.
2. **Warning**: A formal notice clarifying consequences if behavior persists.
3. **Suspension or Removal**: Temporary or permanent bans from Trames repositories and spaces for severe or repeated misconduct.

Moderation exists solely to protect the community from harmful behavior. It is **never** used to resolve technical disputes or suppress good-faith dissent.
